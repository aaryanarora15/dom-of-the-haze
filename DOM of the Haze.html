FIELD MANUAL

1. Press F12 to open DevTools → Elements. The UI is corrupted; the markup is your weapon.
2. Open a second tab of this page as the other station and put both tabs side by side.
3. Radar tab: in Elements, change a sector's data-status from infected to sterilized, or delete its <zombie-node> children. After 30 s an untreated sector mutates and infects its neighbours. Never delete #main-power-grid.
4. Power tab: click PUMP to keep the reactor above 30%, or the Radar tab blacks out. Drag the Purifier Beacon off the right edge of the window: a laser enters the Radar tab from the left and sterilizes the row it crosses.
5. Purple Purge: press PURGE in both tabs within 3 seconds of each other to sterilize everything.
Escape in 4 stages (see the Objective panel). Keep containment above 0%. Also: locked sectors need data-locked set to false first, trapped survivors give +12 containment if you clean their sector in time, and pumping the reactor builds heat (use VENT COOLANT).
